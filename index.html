01

Logistics Performance Analytics

Individual coursework · 2026 · Data Analyst
85,410trips in one master table
7 of 7late trips on route RTE00017
97.66%duration variance explained by distance (R²)

Four separate datasets (loads, trips, routes, customers) had to become one. I filled in missing fleet identifiers, checked outliers with the logistics context in mind rather than blindly, and joined everything into a single table.

Delivery was 99.99% on time, so the interesting part was the exception: every late trip sat on one route. That points to investigating RTE00017, not overhauling the whole system.

Refrigerated and dry van trips earn almost the same average revenue, which suggests pricing should be checked against actual operating cost.

A simple distance-based OLS model predicted duration with a 1.65-hour mean absolute error. I'd be cautious using it for prediction: only 7 late trips, and no external factors like weather or traffic.

Supermarket Sales Data Warehouse

Team of five · 2026 · Data Engineer
CSV1,000 records
PentahoETL
MySQLstar schema
Power BIdashboard

My part was the ETL. Using Pentaho, I split the raw Myanmar supermarket CSV into dimension tables (product, customer, location, time) plus one sales fact table, so the data was tidy enough for Power BI to analyze without workarounds.

Across the 1,000 unique invoices: total revenue of about 323K, a 6.97/10 average rating, and Food & Beverages as the top product line. While rebuilding the dashboard I also found repeated invoice rows in the fact table of my Power BI model (3,380 rows for 1,000 invoices) and de-duplicated them for these figures.
